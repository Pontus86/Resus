# Emergency Room Tileset Description

This tileset is a **concept sheet** showing the visual style and modular
pieces for building hospital rooms in the game. It is intended as a
style guide rather than a production sprite atlas.

## Floor Tiles (Top Left)

Five floor variants: - ER floor - Alternate ER floor - ICU floor -
Operating room floor - Hallway floor

Below them are floor transition strips for joining different room types.

## Wall Tiles (Top Center)

Modular wall sections including: - Straight wall - Inner corner - Outer
corner - T-junction

Designed to connect seamlessly.

## Doors (Top Right)

Includes: - Single door - Wide door - Automatic sliding door - Double
door - Fire exit door

## Glass & Partitions

Contains: - Glass wall - Half-height glass wall - Glass corner - Glass
door - Privacy curtain

Useful for ICU bays and resuscitation rooms.

## Wall Details & Fixtures

Medical wall equipment: - Oxygen outlet - Medical air outlet - Vacuum
outlet - Electrical sockets - Network/data outlets - Hand sanitizer -
Glove dispenser - Paper towel dispenser - Soap dispenser - Whiteboard -
Monitor - Clock - Wall controls

## Cabinets & Storage

Storage furniture including: - Wall cabinets - Base cabinets - Open
shelves - Tall cabinet - Sink cabinet

## Built-in Fixtures

Hospital sinks and counters.

## Ceiling & Overhead

Includes: - Ceiling lights - Ceiling service boom - Ceiling panels -
Security camera

## Signs & Markings

Example room signs, floor markings and colored guide lines.

## Shadow Overlays

Reusable shadow sprites to add depth without redrawing assets.

## Furniture & Props

Static props: - Computer monitor - Workstation - Stool - Office chair -
Shelving - Crash cart - Waste bins - Sharps container - Telephone

## Example Room Layouts

Example assembled emergency rooms demonstrating how the modular tiles
fit together.

## Scale Reference

Shows approximate character and bed scale. Recommended tile size: **64 ×
64 pixels**.

## Notes

This sheet is intended as a **reference for creating production-ready
modular tiles**. Individual assets should eventually be exported as
separate PNG sprites arranged on a fixed grid with consistent pivots and
naming.
